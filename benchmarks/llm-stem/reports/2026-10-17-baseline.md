Hand reproduction of the fwiz-side failures (all confirmed with `./bin/fwiz`):

- `fibs.fw(n=10, result=?)` on a section-only file: `Cannot solve for 'result'`. Same with `--steps`, no hint. Adding `answer = fib(k)` makes `fibt.fw(k=10, answer=?)` and `fibt.fw(answer=55, k=?)` work.
- `fib.fw(n=?min in [0..30], f>=55)`: false "no value satisfies". `f=55` form: `?max/?min requires at least one comparator constraint`.
- `hs.fw(n=?, handshakes=45)`: `n = -9`, `n = 10`; this is correct output, listed only to show why the LLM missed.

## Top friction points

1. Section-only file cannot be queried, error gives no hint. FWIZ-151 (new). Seen in `rec-fibonacci`, `rev-fib-index`. Related: FWIZ-8, FWIZ-137.
2. Sweep reports "infeasible" when evaluation fails. FWIZ-152 (new). Seen in `rev-fib-index`. Related: FWIZ-41.
3. Models forget the run block and the runner ends the attempt (4 of ~27 attempts incl. probe). A harness fix, not fwiz. Added to FWIZ-149.
4. Two-root answers are returned without hinting at domain restrictions. No ticket: fwiz output is correct; the benchmark statement or prompt could ask for "the physical root". Seen in `rev-handshakes`.
5. The 300 s LLM timeout loses attempts (2 of ~27, incl. probe). Added to FWIZ-149.

Not hit: `--integrate` (FWIZ-124) and deferred-identifier errors (FWIZ-8 touched via the vague-error message in point 1).
